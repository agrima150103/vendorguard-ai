CLOUDNOVA SOLUTIONS — SECURITY QUESTIONNAIRE
Completed: March 2025

Q1: Do you encrypt data at rest?
A: Yes. All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Encryption keys are managed
through a managed key service with annual rotation.

Q2: Do you encrypt data in transit?
A: Yes. All data in transit uses TLS 1.3 minimum.

Q3: What is your data retention policy?
A: Active customer data is retained for the contract plus 30 days.

Q4: Do you have an incident response plan?
A: Yes. The plan is reviewed annually and affected customers are notified within
72 hours where applicable. Contact: incidents@cloudnova.io

Q5: Do you conduct third-party penetration testing?
A: Yes. Annual testing is conducted by an independent provider.

Q6: Are you SOC 2 Type II certified?
A: Yes. The latest audit period ended in September 2024.

Q7: What is your backup retention?
A: Encrypted backups are retained for 90 days.
