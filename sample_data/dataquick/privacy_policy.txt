DATAQUICK ANALYTICS — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: November 2024

DataQuick processes names, email addresses, purchase history and behavioural data.
Processed insights may be shared with analytics partners.
Customer data exports are deleted after 12 months.
Deletion requests are completed within 90 days, while anonymised aggregate data may
be retained indefinitely for model training.
A GDPR Data Processing Agreement is still in progress.
Contact: privacy@dataquick.de
